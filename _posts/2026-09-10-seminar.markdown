At 11:00am, room E349, **Tomotaka Kuroda** will be talking about

### Reconsideration of Stochastic Inflation

*We investigate the nonlinear extension of stochastic inflation. At
second order, non-Markovian effects and quadratic noise appear at the
same perturbative order as the usual nonlinear terms. Including both
effects, we derive the three-point functions for a scalar field in de
Sitter spacetime and for slow-roll inflation. On superhorizon scales,
our stochastic results exactly reproduce the corresponding in-in
calculations.*
