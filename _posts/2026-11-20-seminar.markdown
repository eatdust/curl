### Lattice Monte Carlo of quantum field theory on de Sitter space

*Quantum fields in de Sitter space behave in ways that have no
counterpart in flat space. Because of the accelerated expansion,
light fields become strongly coupled on large scales, however weak
their interaction. In particle physics, strongly coupled theories are
studied with lattice Monte Carlo methods, but bringing these tools to
curved spacetime has been a major challenge, because discretizing a
curved geometry typically breaks its symmetries. In this talk I will
introduce a new lattice formulation that keeps the de Sitter
symmetries exact, and show the first Monte Carlo simulations of an
interacting scalar field on Euclidean de Sitter space. At weak
coupling, the simulations reproduce the equilibrium of stochastic
inflation to a few parts per thousand, without ever invoking a
Langevin equation. Small deviations from this distribution closely
follow the one-loop renormalization of the coupling. At strong
coupling, the effective interaction saturates while the field
distribution stays close to the stochastic prediction. This
saturation is the de Sitter counterpart of the mechanism behind the
lattice bound on the Higgs mass in flat space. More broadly, this new
discretization opens up de Sitter space to lattice Monte Carlo
methods.*
