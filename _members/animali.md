My research focuses on the theoretical cosmology of the primordial
universe, with a particular emphasis on cosmic inflation, its
modelling, and its predictions.

This includes the formation of primordial black holes from large
density fluctuations and the associated gravitational-wave background,
both of which provide valuable insights into the universe’s origins
and initial conditions. In this context, I am especially interested in
the use of effective methods to describe the backreaction of quantum
fields on the inflationary background, such as the stochastic
inflation formalism.

Additionally, I am also keen on more formal aspects of quantum field
theory in curved spacetime.
